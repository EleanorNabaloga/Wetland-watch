Signals are not cases

Anonymous voices reveal hotspots. They never trigger enforcement on their own.

Consent before contact

NEMA only contacts a reporter who has said yes, through a channel they chose.

People decide

No auto-rejection. No machine translation as evidence. Every decision has a reason and an appeal.

Sized to capacity

Cases reach inspection at the pace NEMA can absorb. Nothing is dropped silently.

Track B: Evidence-grade case

Explicit consent
Identity
Kept in a separate encrypted vault, never on the case record.
Evidence
Signed at capture: photo, GPS accuracy, timestamp, device signature. Or a voice recording on the 0800 line.
Used for
A human-reviewed case that can be queued for inspection.
Feedback
Received, Assigned, Action taken, or No action with a reason.
Rewards
Community recognition, a public thank-you, or lottery-based airtime after verification. Never cash per case.

Pick the channel that works for you

What happens after you report?

You get honest updates in plain words. Messages never name a site or a person, and they can be worded so anyone glancing at your phone learns nothing.

    The message you would get

    Reporter protection

    • No retaliation, and a safe channel to tell us if it happens.
    • NEMA contacts you only after you consent.
    • Inspectors never say what prompted a visit.
    • Feedback never names sites or alleged violators.
    • Legal support is available if you need it.
    • Linking a phone number to a case is restricted and logged.

    Recognition, not payment

    After a case is verified you can choose community recognition, a public thank-you, or entry into a lottery for airtime. We never pay cash per case, because that rewards volume over truth. Signals earn no reward.

      Pilot design

      Six to twelve months, three sites, a control, and results judged against thresholds fixed before any data arrives. We scale only when the thresholds are met and NEMA has more capacity to absorb the work.

      Lubigi wetland

      Urban-edge site with the strongest existing pressure. Anchors the pilot.

      One rural wetland

      Tests basic-phone and IVR reach where connectivity is thin. Site to be chosen with NEMA.

      One urban site

      Tests smartphone capture and dense reporting. Site to be chosen with NEMA.

      Baseline data is collected first at every site, alongside a comparable control site with no platform.

      1. Set-up

        MoU, DPIA, access model, baseline and control. Thresholds pre-registered.

      2. Live pilot, months 3 to 9 or 12

        Both tracks at all three sites. Transparency reports on schedule.

      3. Scale decision

        Thresholds met and capacity up: expand. Otherwise fix the bottleneck first.
